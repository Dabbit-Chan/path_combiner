## 1.0.0

* Added **Icon to Path**: convert any `IconData` to a `Path` with `IconData.toPath()`.
* Added **Text to Path**: convert any `String` to a `Path` with `String.toPath()`.
* Added `PaintingStyle` support to `PathCombiner`: switch between `PaintingStyle.fill` and `PaintingStyle.stroke`.
* Improved the example app and README documentation.
* Fixed known issues.
